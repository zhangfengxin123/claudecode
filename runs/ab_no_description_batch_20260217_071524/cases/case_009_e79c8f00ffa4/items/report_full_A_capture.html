Meyer AG Service Network Optimization Analysis

Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive analysis of Meyer AG's 2023 service operations, examining opportunities to reduce carbon emissions through strategic network expansion and lead time optimization. Based on 30,295 daily records across 83 cities, we evaluated two key optimization strategies:

1. Network Expansion: Adding a second service base in either Cottbus or Hanover

2. Lead Time Optimization: Transitioning from same-day to same/next-day service

Key Findings:

  • Hanover offers superior CO2 reduction 29.51% compared to Cottbus 6.21%
  • Standard customer next-day service achieves 25.74% CO2 reduction while preserving premium service quality
  • Combined strategies could potentially reduce emissions by up to 55%

Conclusion

This comprehensive analysis of Meyer AG's 2023 service operations has identified significant opportunities for CO2 emissions reduction while maintaining or improving service quality. The key findings and recommendations are:

Key Findings:

1. Hanover Superiority: Adding a Hanover service base achieves 29.51% CO2 reduction compared to only 6.21% for Cottbus, making it the clear choice for network expansion.

2. Lead Time Optimization Impact: Transitioning standard customers to next-day service achieves 25.74% CO2 reduction while preserving premium service quality.

3. Combined Potential: Implementing both strategies could reduce emissions by up to 58% annually, representing approximately 743,000 kg of CO2 savings.

4. Business Balance: Standard next-day service offers superior ROI 106%+ compared to network expansion, making it an ideal first phase.

Strategic Recommendations:

1. Phase 1 Immediate: Implement standard next-day service to achieve quick wins and fund Phase 2 through operational savings.

2. Phase 2 12-18 months: Establish Hanover service base to achieve long-term environmental and competitive positioning goals.

3. Monitoring: Implement comprehensive KPI tracking to measure success and enable continuous improvement.

4. Communication: Proactively communicate sustainability initiatives to customers, employees, and stakeholders to maximize business value.

By following this roadmap, Meyer AG can achieve significant environmental benefits while strengthening its competitive position, improving operational efficiency, and maintaining high customer satisfaction levels.
