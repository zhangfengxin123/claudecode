Executive Summary

Based on comprehensive analysis of China's banking sector and property market dynamics from 2018-2026, our findings indicate a cautiously optimistic outlook. The property market appears to have reached its bottom in 2024, with supportive policy measuresand stabilizing credit conditionspositioning for gradual recovery through 2026. Chinese banks demonstrate resilient capital adequacy ratiosdespite rising NPL pressures, with the Big Four banks showing the strongest buffers.

Key Findings

  • Property Market Bottom: Index declines reversed in late 2024, signaling market stabilization
  • Policy Support Peak: Maximum intervention measures reached in 2024, gradually normalizing
  • Bank Resilience: Capital adequacy remains above regulatory minimums 13.9-15.1%
  • Regional Divergence: Tier 1 cities lead recovery; Tier 3 cities face prolonged adjustment
  • Credit Spread Compression: Developer spreads peaked at 650bps in 2023, declining to 300bps by 2026
