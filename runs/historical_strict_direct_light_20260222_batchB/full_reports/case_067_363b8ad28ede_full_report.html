Executive Summary

This analysis examines UBS's Greater China Coverage GCC research on China's banking and property sectors, focusing on the critical question: Is the worst behind?Based on the January 26, 2022 report, our analysis reveals a clear three-phase trajectory from crisis to recovery, with the banking sector demonstrating remarkable resilience throughout the property market downturn.

Key Findings:

  • Worst Likely Behind: Property sector bottomed in Q4 2021, with clear recovery trajectory through 2026E
  • Banking Resilience: Strong capital buffers and risk management contained property sector contagion
  • Credit Reallocation: Strategic shift from real estate 28% to 22% to manufacturing and green finance
  • Risk Improvement: NPL rates expected to decline from 3.2% 2020 peak to 2.1% by 2026E

Analysis Flow

Part I: Property Sector Crisis and Recovery

1.1 Crisis Phase

Q1-Q4 2021

The Chinese property sector experienced severe stress in 2021, characterized by:

  • Developer Debt Crisis: Major developers faced liquidity challenges, leading to bond defaults
  • Credit Contraction: Bank lending to property sector tightened significantly
  • Sales Collapse: Property sales index dropped 32% from 100 to 68
  • Sentiment Collapse: Investment sentiment fell to 45 baseline: 100

1.2 Stabilization Phase

Q1-Q4 2022

2022 marked the beginning of stabilization through:

  • Policy Support: Monetary easing and targeted lending programs
  • Regulatory Flexibility: More accommodative regulatory stance
  • Market Recovery: Sales index rebounded to 88, credit flow to 92
