Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive analysis of heating oil consumption data spanning from 2004 to 2017. The dataset contains 71 records, with 31 valid consumption periods analyzed. Over the 13-year period, total oil consumption reached 36,625 liters, with an average daily consumption of 25.55 liters. The analysis reveals significant variations in consumption patterns, with daily rates ranging from 4.98 to 61.76 liters, indicating factors such as seasonal variations, heating system efficiency changes, and usage patterns.

Key Findings

  • Total Consumption: 36,625 liters over 13 years
  • Average Daily Rate: 25.55 L/day Median: 20.0 L/day
  • Peak Consumption Period: 3,000 liters in a single period 2007-02-10
  • Daily Rate Variability: High standard deviation of 14.76 L/day
  • Data Coverage: 2004-11-14 to 2017-12-04
