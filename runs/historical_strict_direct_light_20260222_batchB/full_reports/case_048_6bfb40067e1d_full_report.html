House Price Prediction Analysis: Comprehensive Data-Driven Insights

Executive Summary

This comprehensive analysis examines housing market data to understand key factors driving house prices and develop a predictive model. Based on 1,460 property records, we identify strong correlations between physical attributes and sale prices, with overall quality rating emerging as the most significant predictor. Our linear regression model achieves 82.7% accuracy R² in predicting prices, providing valuable insights for real estate valuation and investment decisions.

Key Findings:

  • Average sale price: 180,921 with median at 163,000
  • Price range: 34,900 to 755,000
  • Overall Quality rating explains 79% of price variation
  • Model achieves R² = 0.827 with mean absolute error of 22,313

Data Overview

The dataset contains 1,460 residential property sales with 81 features covering:

  • Physical Attributes: Living area, lot size, basement, garage, room counts
  • Quality Ratings: Overall quality, condition scores, material quality
  • Temporal Data: Year built, year remodeled, sale dates
  • Location: Neighborhood information
  • Amenities: Fireplaces, porches, pools, fences

Predictive Modeling

Linear Regression Model

We developed a linear regression model using 13 key features to predict house prices. The model was trained on 80% of the data 1,162 samples and validated on 20% 290 samples.

Model Performance:

  • Training R²: 0.779 77.9% variance explained
  • Test R²: 0.827 82.7% variance explained
  • Test MAE: 22,313 average prediction error
  • Test RMSE: 32,504 root mean squared error

The model performs slightly better on test data, indicating good generalization and absence of overfitting.

Model Limitations and Recommendations

Current Limitations

1. Linear Assumption: Real estate pricing has non-linear components thresholds, diminishing returns

2. Feature Interactions: Quality may interact with size high quality in small homes vs. low quality in large homes

3. Geographic Features: Neighborhood dummies not included in current model

4. Missing Features: Location proximity, school districts, lot shape not utilized

Improvement Opportunities

1. Feature Engineering:

Add age features Current Year - YearBuilt

Create interaction terms Quality × Size

Include neighborhood dummy variables

2. Advanced Modeling:

Gradient Boosting XGBoost, LightGBM

Random Forest for non-linear relationships

Neural networks for complex interactions

3. Data Enhancement:

Economic indicators interest rates, unemployment

Geographic data distance to amenities

Market conditions at time of sale
