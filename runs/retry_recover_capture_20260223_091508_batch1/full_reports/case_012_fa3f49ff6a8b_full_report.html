Executive Summary

This report analyzes the World Economic Forum's Future of Jobs Report 2025 economy profiles, examining 20 economies across Asia, Europe, the Americas, and the Middle East. The analysis reveals critical insights into how AI strategy, skill disruption, and workforce transformation priorities correlate with labor market outcomes. Key findings include a significant negative correlation between skill disruption and net job growth -0.491, p=0.028, and a strong positive correlation between DEI priorities and skill disruption +0.574, p=0.008, suggesting that economies prioritizing diversity and inclusion are more aggressively transforming their workforces.
