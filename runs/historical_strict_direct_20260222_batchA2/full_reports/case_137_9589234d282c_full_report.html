Online Retail Sales Analytics 2009-2011

Executive Summary

This comprehensive analysis examines online retail transactions from December 2009 to December 2011, spanning over 1.06 million raw records. After data cleaning, we analyzed 805,549 valid transactions totaling £17.7 million in revenue. The analysis reveals significant insights about customer behavior, seasonal patterns, and geographic distribution that can drive strategic business decisions.

Key Findings:

  • The United Kingdom dominates revenue with 83% of total sales £14.7M
  • Peak shopping hours are 10:00-12:00 and 14:00-15:00
  • Strong seasonal patterns with Q4 October-November showing highest sales
  • Top-performing product: REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER £286,486
  • Forecasting model predicts 114% growth in the next 90 days
