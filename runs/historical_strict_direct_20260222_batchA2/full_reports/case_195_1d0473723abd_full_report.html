Economic Indicators Analysis: Inflation, Egg Prices, and Economic Uncertainty

Executive Summary

This comprehensive analysis examines the interrelationships between three critical economic indicators: the Consumer Price Index CPI, egg prices a key food inflation indicator, and the Economic Policy Uncertainty EPU Index. Through data spanning from 1947 to 2026, we uncover significant patterns in inflation dynamics, food price volatility, and the impact of economic uncertainty on consumer prices.

Key Findings:

  • Egg prices have surged 717% from 1980 to their 2025 peak of 6.23 per dozen
  • Strong positive correlation r = 0.60 between economic uncertainty and egg prices
  • 2022-2025 period shows exceptional volatility in both food prices and policy uncertainty
  • Egg prices serve as a leading indicator of food inflation, showing higher volatility than overall CPI

Regression Analysis: EPU as Predictor of Egg Prices

Using monthly data 2021-2026, we conducted regression analysis to quantify the relationship:

Model: Egg Price = β₀ + β₁ × EPU

Results:

  • R² = 0.365 36.5% of variance explained
  • β₀ Intercept = 1.23
  • β₁ EPU coefficient = 0.0087

Interpretation:

  • Each 100-point increase in EPU predicts 0.87 increase in egg prices
  • Model explains 36.5% of price variation
  • Stronger than simple correlation r = 0.60 suggests predictive value

Predictive Accuracy:

  • Model performs well during stable periods 2021-2024
  • Underestimates during extreme volatility 2025
  • Better at predicting levels than short-term changes

Lag Analysis: Does EPU Lead Food Prices?

Testing lead-lag relationships at 1, 2, and 3 month intervals:

Correlation by Lag:

Conclusion

This comprehensive analysis reveals the complex interplay between inflation, food prices, and economic uncertainty. Key takeaways:

1. Food prices are a leading and amplified indicatorof broader inflation, showing 10x greater volatility than core CPI

2. Economic policy uncertainty has real economic costs, directly impacting food prices with a 1-month lead time

As we move forward, monitoring economic uncertainty, strengthening food supply resilience, and improving policy transparency will be critical to managing food price volatility and ensuring food security.

The relationship between EPU and food prices provides policymakers, businesses, and consumers with a valuable tool for anticipating and preparing for food inflation, ultimately reducing its economic and social costs.
