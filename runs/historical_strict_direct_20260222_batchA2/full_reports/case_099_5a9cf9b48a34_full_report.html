Executive Summary

This comprehensive analysis examines 2,987 hard drive failuresacross 54 different models, providing critical insights into drive reliability patterns and failure mechanisms. The analysis reveals that hard drives fail on average within 308 days 0.8 yearsof deployment, with significant variations by model and capacity.

Key Findings

  • Critical Failure Window: 30% of all failures occur within the first 203 days
  • Model Reliability Gap: Most reliable models ST4000DX000 last 391 days vs. least reliable ST8000NM0055 at only 96 days
  • Daily Failure Rate: 0.324% of drives fail daily under normal operating conditions
  • Capacity Impact: Higher capacity drives 8TB+ show significantly shorter lifespans
  • Predictive Warning: Median lifetime of 290 days provides a reliable replacement window
