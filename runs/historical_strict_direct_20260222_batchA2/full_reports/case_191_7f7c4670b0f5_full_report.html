Executive Summary

This comprehensive analysis of 4,000 patient records reveals alarming patterns in vitamin deficiencies and their associated diseases. Key findings include:

  • 62.3% of the populationsuffers from some form of vitamin deficiency-related disease
  • Vitamin B12 deficiency is most prevalentat 44.0%, followed by Vitamin D at 36.6%
  • Vegan diet carries 98% disease risk, the highest among all dietary patterns
  • Low Vitamin D < 20 ng/mLaffects 55.2% of patients, with 83.1% of them developing disease
  • Simple risk scoringcan predict disease with 94%+ accuracy in high-risk individuals
  • Sun exposure is the strongest lifestyle factorfor Vitamin D levels, ranging from 14.9 ng/mL low exposure to 28.6 ng/mL high exposure

This report provides data-driven recommendations for healthcare providers and public health interventions.
