Commercial Reusable Rocket Market Analysis 2025

Executive Summary

The commercial aerospace industry is undergoing a revolutionary transformation driven by reusable rocket technology and massive satellite constellation deployment plans. This report analyzes the market landscape, highlighting:

  • Cost Revolution: Rocket launch costs have the potential to drop from 110-180M expendable to 20-50M fully reusable, a 70-86% reduction
  • Market Validation: SpaceX demonstrated 10.6x launch growth 13→138 launches/year and 4.4x reuse rate improvement 1.67→7.33 times from 2018-2024

The industry is transitioning from the "0-to-1" phase to a golden period of industrialization and commercialization.

The reuse of first-stage hardware alone captures 77.8% of hardware costs, making it the primary target for reusability efforts.

Industry Ecosystem

Value Chain Flow

The commercial rocket industry encompasses a complex value chain:

Upstream Supply Chain:

  • Materials: Metals, composites for rocket structures
  • Components: Electronics, sensors, specialized parts

Midstream Manufacturing:

  • Engine Manufacturing: New materials Sirui, Western Materials; Component processing Bright Laser, Hangyu, Pike, AVIC; Assembly Aerospace Power
  • Structure Manufacturing: Arrow body processing Superjet, Guanglian, China National Precision
  • Control Systems: Components Hongyuan, Aerospace Electric, AVIC Optoelectronics; Cables Hualing, Quanxin; Modules Aerospace Electronics, Polytechnic Navigation, Zhimingda

Launch Services:

  • Launch Sites: Hainan Commercial Space Launch Site now operational
  • Launch Insurance: Critical for commercial operations
  • Range Services: Telemetry, tracking, safety

Downstream Applications:

  • Satellite Internet: Primary growth driver
  • Communication: Traditional satellite services
  • Remote Sensing: Earth observation
  • Navigation: Positioning services

Reusability Value Flow

Reusable Rocket Lifecycle:

1. Manufacturing: Engine 60%, Structure 17.8%, Control 22.4%, Propellant 0.7%

2. Launch: Primary mission execution

3. Separation: Stage 1 recovery begins, Stage 2 expended

4. Atmospheric Return: Reentry burn and thermal management

5. Landing: Vertical landing using propulsion

6. Inspection: Non-destructive testing and evaluation

7. Refurbishment: Engine refurbishment 25M + other components 475M = 500M

8. Reuse: Return to launch queue

Economic Impact:

  • Expendable: 50M/launch new manufacturing required
  • Reusable: 15M/launch Stage 2 manufacturing + refurbishment
  • Savings: 35M/launch = 70% cost reduction
