Auto Sales Data Analysis: Strategic Insights and Performance Review 2018-2020

Executive Summary

This comprehensive analysis examines auto sales data from January 2018 through May 2020, encompassing 2,747 transactions across 19 countries and 7 product lines. The total sales volume reached 9.76 million, with a remarkable growth trajectory showing a 39% increase from 2018 to 2019. Key findings reveal that Classic Carsdominate product sales 3.84M, 39% of total, the USAleads geographic markets 3.36M, 34% of total, and Medium-sized dealsgenerate the majority of revenue 5.93M, 61% of total. The analysis identifies strategic opportunities for market expansion, product line optimization, and customer retention initiatives.
