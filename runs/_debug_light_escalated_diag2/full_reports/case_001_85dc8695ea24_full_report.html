Executive Summary

This comprehensive analysis examines UK online retail sales data spanning December 2009 through December 2011, encompassing two fiscal years with over 1,067,371 transactions. The dataset reveals a thriving e-commerce operation with £19.29 millionin total sales across 43 international markets.

Key highlights include:

  • Strong year-over-year growth: 2010 saw £9.49M in sales, while 2011 achieved £9.00M
