Banking Performance Analysis Report

Executive Summary

This report provides a comprehensive analysis of banking performance data spanning 14 months from December 2024 to January 2026. The dataset includes 4,676 performance records across 83 institutions and 334 employees, tracking key metrics such as deposit sales, insurance sales, and customer growth.

Key Findings:

  • Total combined sales volume: ¥38.4 billion Deposit: ¥34.9B, Insurance: ¥3.6B
  • Average monthly sales: ¥2.7 billion with relative stability across the period
  • Top 5 employees contributed ¥971.5 million in total sales 2.5% of total
  • L1 employees outperform L2 employees across both deposit and insurance sales
  • Customer growth shows a slight declining trend over the period

Strategic Recommendations

1. Customer Acquisition Focus

The declining customer growth trend 7.2% decline over 14 months is concerning. Immediate action required:

  • Implement targeted customer acquisition campaigns
  • Investigate top-performing institutions' customer acquisition strategies
  • Consider incentive programs for customer acquisition

2. L2 Employee Development

L2 employees show 98% lower average performance than L1:

  • Implement mentorship programs pairing L2 with top L1 performers
  • Provide additional training on high-value client management
  • Review L2 promotion criteria to ensure effective performance progression

3. Product Mix Optimization

Insurance represents only 9.3% of sales despite higher variability:

  • Cross-selling opportunities exist to increase insurance penetration
  • Consider training programs to boost insurance sales competency
  • Analyze customer segments with low insurance adoption

4. Performance Consistency

Top 10 institutions represent only 16.84% of total sales, suggesting opportunities:

  • Share best practices from top-performing institutions
  • Implement performance benchmarking across all 83 institutions
  • Create peer learning networks

5. Negative Value Investigation

20% of records show negative sales values:

  • Analyze patterns in refunds/cancellations
  • Identify root causes product quality, service issues, customer fit
  • Implement preventive measures to reduce negative transactions

Data Quality Notes

The dataset contains approximately 19-20% negative values across sales metrics and customer growth. While these likely represent valid business events refunds, cancellations, customer churn, further investigation is recommended to understand:

  • The root causes of negative transactions
  • Geographic or institutional patterns
  • Seasonal trends in negative values

Conclusion

The banking performance analysis reveals a stable core business with strong total sales volume ¥38.4B over 14 months but concerning trends in customer acquisition. Performance is highly concentrated among top employees and institutions, with L1 employees significantly outperforming L2 counterparts.

Key priorities for management:

1. Reverse the declining customer growth trend

2. Develop L2 employees to close the performance gap with L1

3. Share best practices from top-performing institutions

4. Investigate and reduce negative transaction patterns

5. Leverage the strong correlation between deposit and insurance sales for cross-selling opportunities

With targeted interventions in these areas, the banking network can build on its stable foundation to achieve sustainable growth.
