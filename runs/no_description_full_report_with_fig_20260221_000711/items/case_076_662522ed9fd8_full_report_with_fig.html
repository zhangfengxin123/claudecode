This report presents a comprehensive analysis of an A/B testing experiment evaluating different pricing strategies for monthly subscription cards. The experiment compares a control group Group A with three test groups B1, B2, B3 to assess the impact of pricing changes on key performance metrics.

Statistical Considerations

Sample Size Impact:

  • Group A: Large sample n=122,216 → High statistical power, reliable estimates
  • Group B1: Moderate sample n=73,221 → Reasonable reliability
  • Group B2: Small sample n=24,327 → Higher variance, less reliable
  • Group B3: Small sample n=24,631 → Higher variance, less reliable

Confidence in Results:

1. High Confidence: Group A baseline metrics, Group B1 results

2. Moderate Confidence: Group B2 and B3 payment rates

3. Low Confidence: Group B2 and B3 ARPU/ARPPU differences

Methodological Limitations

1. Sample Size Disparity

  • Direct revenue comparisons are misleading
  • Per-user metrics more reliable but still affected by sample variance
  • Need normalization or weighted analysis

2. Unknown Experimental Duration

  • unclear how long each group was exposed to pricing
  • Short-term effects may differ from long-term user behavior
  • Seasonal variations not accounted for

3. Lack of Cohort Analysis

  • Cannot track user behavior over time
  • Unable to measure retention, churn, or lifetime value
  • First-time vs. returning users not distinguished

4. Missing Context

  • Price points for each group not provided
  • User demographics and segmentation unknown
  • Competitive landscape and market conditions unclear
