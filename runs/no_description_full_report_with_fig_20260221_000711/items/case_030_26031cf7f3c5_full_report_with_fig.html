Meesho Supplier Returns Analysis: A Deep Dive into RTO Patterns

Executive Summary

This analysis examines 789 return-to-origin RTO transactions processed through Meesho's supplier network between August 2024 and April 2025. The data reveals critical patterns in return cycles, category performance, and seasonal trends that can inform operational improvements.

Key Findings:

  • 91.4% of returnscome from a single category Cups, Mugs & Saucers
  • Average return cycleis 16.78 days with minimal variation σ = 5.78 days
  • Peak return volumeoccurred in January 2025 165 returns
  • 53% of returnscomplete within 14-20 days, indicating predictable cycle times
  • Declining trendin return volume observed from February 2025 onwards

Insights

Dominance of Cups, Mugs & Saucers

The overwhelming concentration of returns 91.4% in a single category suggests several potential causes:

  • High volume category: This category likely represents a significant portion of total orders
  • Fragility concerns: Glass and ceramic products may have higher damage rates during transit
  • Product-specific issues: Possible quality control or packaging concerns specific to this category
  • Customer expectations: Higher return expectations for kitchenware due to aesthetic preferences

Consistent Return Durations Across Categories

Despite the volume disparity, return durations remain remarkably consistent across all categories:

  • All categories average between 11-17 days
  • Jars & Containers show the longest average duration 17.12 days
  • Minimal variation suggests consistent processing times regardless of product type

Actionable Recommendations

2. Packaging optimization: Review packaging standards for fragile items

3. Product quality review: Conduct quality audits for high-return categories

4. Customer education: Improve product descriptions and imagery to reduce mismatch expectations
