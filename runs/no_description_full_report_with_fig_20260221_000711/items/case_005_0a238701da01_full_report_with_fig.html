Comprehensive Analysis of Gold Factors and Economic Indicators

2010-2025

Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive analysis of gold-related economic indicators and macroeconomic factors spanning from January 2010 to December 2025. The dataset encompasses 192 monthly observations covering eight key variables: central bank gold reserves, budget deficits, interest income ratios, exchange rates CHF/USD and JPY/USD, the OFR Financial Stress Index, USD reserve shares, and other critical economic indicators.

Key Findings:

  • Strong Gold-Reserve Relationship: Central bank gold reserves show a remarkably strong positive correlation 0.86 with JPY/USD exchange rates, indicating synchronized movements between gold holdings and Japanese yen valuation.
  • USD Reserve Decline: USD reserve share demonstrates a significant negative correlation -0.68 with gold reserves, suggesting an inverse relationship as central banks diversify away from dollar-denominated assets.
  • Financial Stress Patterns: The OFR Financial Stress Index reveals distinct stress periods, particularly during 2011, 2020 COVID-19 pandemic, and 2022-2023 periods.
  • Exchange Rate Divergence: Swiss Franc and Japanese Yen show divergent trends against USD, reflecting different monetary policy responses and economic conditions.

The analysis provides actionable insights for investors, policymakers, and financial institutions seeking to understand the complex interrelationships between gold reserves and broader economic indicators.
