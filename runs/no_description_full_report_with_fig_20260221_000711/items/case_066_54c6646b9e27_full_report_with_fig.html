Alphabet Q4 2025 Earnings Analysis: Record Revenue and AI-Powered Growth

Executive Summary

Alphabet delivered a stellar Q4 2025, achieving a historic milestone with annual revenues exceeding 400 billion for the first time. The company demonstrated robust growth across all core segments, with particular strength in Google Cloud 48% YoY and sustained momentum in Search 17% YoY. AI investments are beginning to yield tangible results, evidenced by Gemini App reaching 750 million monthly active users and processing 10+ billion tokens per minute.

Key Highlights:

  • Q4 Revenue: 113.8B +18% YoY
  • FY 2025 Revenue: 402.8B +15% YoY - First time exceeding $400B
  • Net Income: 34.5B +30% YoY
  • Google Cloud Revenue: 17.7B +48% YoY with expanding margins 30.1%
  • Paid Subscriptions: 325M+ across consumer services
  • 2026 CapEx Guidance: 175-185B reflecting massive AI infrastructure investments

The quarter showcases Alphabet's successful dual strategy: maintaining core business strength while aggressively investing in AI infrastructure that drives new growth vectors.

Alphabet delivered 35.9B in operating income for Q4 2025, representing a 16% year-over-year increase. The operating margin of 31.6% remained essentially stable down 0.5pp from 32.1%, demonstrating excellent cost control despite significant AI investments.

2. Google Cloud 5.3B, +154% YoY: The most dramatic margin expansion, with operating margin improving from 17.5% to 30.1%. This margin expansion signals that Google Cloud has achieved critical scale and operating leverage, with enterprise AI solutions contributing to improved unit economics.
