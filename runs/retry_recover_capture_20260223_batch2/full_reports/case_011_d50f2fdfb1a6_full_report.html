Summer Olympics 1896-2024: A Comprehensive Data Analysis

Executive Summary

This comprehensive analysis examines 128 years of Summer Olympic Games from 1896 to 2024, analyzing 252,565 athlete records across 234 participating nations and 76 sports. The analysis reveals remarkable growth from 241 athletes in 1896 to over 15,000 in recent games, alongside a dramatic shift toward gender equality with female participation increasing from 0% to nearly 50%. The United States dominates all-time medal counts with 2,583 medals, while China leads in gold medal efficiency at 41.7%. Predictive modeling suggests continued growth in participation, with Olympic gender equality projected to be achieved within the next decade.

This analysis follows a systematic data science workflow, processing raw Olympic data through cleaning, aggregation, statistical analysis, and machine learning to extract actionable insights.
