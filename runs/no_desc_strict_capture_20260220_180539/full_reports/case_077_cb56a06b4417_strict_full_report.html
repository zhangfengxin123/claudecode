Intel Q4 2025 Financial Analysis: Revenue Decline and Strategic Turnaround

Executive Summary

Intel Corporation reported Q4 2025 revenue of 13.7 billion, representing a 4% year-over-year declinefrom 14.2 billion in Q4 2024. The full-year FY 2025 revenue reached 52.9 billion, a 2% YoY decreasefrom FY 2024's 54.2 billion. Despite revenue contraction, Intel achieved non-GAAP EPS of 0.15for Q4 and 0.42for FY 2025, while GAAP EPS was -0.18for Q4 due to significant impairment charges. The analysis reveals critical challenges in the Foundry business -55.7% operating marginbut demonstrates the strength of the Client Computing Group CCG, 27% OMand Data Center & AI DCAI, 26.4% OMsegments. The company launched the IDM 2.0 transformationwith 10 billion in cost savings targets, forecasting a breakeven EBITDA for Foundry by 2027.

1.1 Quarterly Revenue Trend

Intel's Q4 2025 revenue of 13.7 billion continues a declining trend, down 4% YoY and slightly below the analyst consensus range of 14.0-14.5 billion. The sequential decline from Q3 2025 14.3 billion reflects softening PC demand and the company's ongoing strategic pivot away from lower-margin businesses.

2. Earnings Per Share

EPS Analysis

2.1 Non-GAAP vs. GAAP Performance

Intel reported a significant divergence between non-GAAP and GAAP EPS:

  • Q4 Non-GAAP EPS: 0.15vs. 0.47 in Q4 2024
  • FY Non-GAAP EPS: 0.42vs. 0.97 in FY 2024
  • Q4 GAAP EPS: -0.18vs. -0.16 in Q4 2024
  • FY GAAP EPS: -1.64vs. 0.45 in FY 2024

The GAAP losses were driven by:

  • 3.7 billion impairment chargeon Foundry assets mostly goodwill
  • 1.9 billion restructuring chargesrelated to cost reduction initiatives
  • 0.8 billion inventory write-downsreflecting weak demand

2.2 Earnings Quality Assessment

The non-GAAP adjustments for Q4 2025 totaled 4.2 billion, primarily:

1. Asset impairments 3.7B- One-time charges on Foundry goodwill and intangibles

2. Restructuring 1.9B- Workforce reduction and facility consolidation

3. Inventory write-downs 0.8B- Aligning inventory with demand outlook

While non-GAAP shows continuing profitability, the GAAP decline indicates the real economic cost of the strategic pivot to IDM 2.0.
