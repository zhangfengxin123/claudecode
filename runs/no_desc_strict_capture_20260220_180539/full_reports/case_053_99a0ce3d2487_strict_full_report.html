Available Truck Carriers Contact Analysis

Executive Summary

This report provides a comprehensive analysis of available truck carriers' contact information, examining communication channel preferences and data quality metrics. The analysis covers 2,138 carriers across three contact categories: both email and phone, phone-only, and email-only contacts.

Key Findings

  • Dominant Channel: 85.9% of carriers 1,837 out of 2,138 provide both email and phone contact information
  • Channel Preference: Phone contact methods show slightly higher average volumes 2.37 compared to email 2.20
  • Data Quality: Good uniqueness ratios 80-90% for both email and phone contacts
  • Outlier Detection: Significant outliers detected with maximum values reaching 111 contacts per carrier
  • Minimal Single-Channel Usage: Only 14.1% of carriers use a single contact method
