Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive metabolomic analysis of human cachexia, a complex metabolic syndrome characterized by progressive muscle wasting. Using untargeted metabolomics profiling across 63 metabolites in 77 individuals 47 cachexia, 30 control, we identified 54 significantly altered metabolitesFDR-corrected p < 0.05, all showing upregulationin cachexic patients. The analysis reveals profound metabolic dysregulation across multiple biochemical pathways, providing potential biomarkers and therapeutic targets for this devastating condition.

Key Findings:

  • 100% of significant metabolites are upregulatedin cachexia 54/54
  • Most significant alteration:Quinolinate ↑2.73x, p = 1.5×10⁻⁵
  • Strongest pathway disruption:Energy metabolism and amino acid catabolism
  • PCA separation:PC1 captures 40.43% variance, showing clear group separation
  • Metabolic signature:Elevated markers of muscle breakdown, oxidative stress, and altered energy metabolism
