Executive Summary

The Chinese internet and new media landscape continued to evolve in December 2025, with short-form video maintaining its dominance while e-commerce and social applications showed steady growth. This analysis examines key trends across major app categories, user engagement metrics, and market dynamics based on Nomura's Monthly App Tracker report.

Key Findings

  • Short Video Leadership: Douyin and short-form video apps maintain market dominance with 965M MAU
  • E-commerce Momentum: Taobao and Pinduoduo show strong growth, with PDD leading at 22.1% YoY growth
  • Live Streaming Decline: Momo and YY Live face continued headwinds with negative growth trends
  • Rising Competition: Emerging apps gaining traction in specific verticals including online reading and mobile games
  • Time Spent Shifts: User attention increasingly concentrated in short-form video and social applications

Category Deep Dives

Short Form Video

The short-form video category demonstrates remarkable resilience and growth potential:

Market Dynamics

  • Douyin maintains market leadership with 965M MAU
  • Kuaishou growing faster at 15.5% YoY, potentially capturing different demographics
  • Category-wide 7% YoY growth despite penetration rates above 90%

User Engagement

  • 43.8 hours per user per month - highest across all categories
  • 3.0% quarterly growth in time spent shows increasing stickiness
  • Algorithm-driven content discovery continues to improve retention

Investment Implications

  • Category remains attractive for continued investment
  • Monetization opportunities in e-commerce integration and creator economy
  • International expansion potential for leading platforms

E-commerce

E-commerce shows healthy momentum with strong performance across major platforms:

Platform Performance

  • Taobao: 910M MAU, 4.2% YoY growth - steady but maturing
  • Meituan: 580M MAU, 8.9% YoY growth - local services expansion
  • Pinduoduo: 550M MAU, 22.1% YoY growth - rapid market share gains

Growth Drivers

  • Live commerce integration driving user engagement
  • Lower-tier market penetration
  • Cross-border e-commerce expansion

Key Trends

  • Increasing competition among top platforms
  • Focus on user experience and logistics optimization
  • Growing importance of social commerce features

Social

Social applications show mixed performance with platform differentiation:

WeChat Dominance

  • 930M MAU, 2.5% YoY growth
  • Near-universal penetration across demographics
  • Expanding beyond messaging into payments, mini-programs, services

QQ Decline

  • 620M MAU, -3.8% YoY growth
  • Demographic shift toward WeChat and newer platforms
  • Challenges in attracting younger users

Category Challenges

  • Market saturation limiting growth potential
  • Competition for user attention from other categories
  • Need for innovation to drive next wave of growth

Live Streaming

Live streaming faces significant headwinds and structural challenges:

Declining Performance

  • Momo: 380M MAU, -8.5% YoY growth
  • YY Live: 320M MAU, -12.3% YoY growth

Structural Challenges

  • Competition from short-form video platforms
  • Shifting user preferences toward on-demand content
  • Regulatory pressures on content and monetization

Category Outlook

  • Continued consolidation expected
  • Niche opportunities in specific verticals e.g., gaming, education
  • Need for differentiation from short-form video

Conclusion

The Chinese internet and new media market continues to evolve rapidly, with clear winners and losers emerging across categories. Short-form video and e-commerce show strong growth momentum, while traditional social and live streaming platforms face increasing challenges. Investors and operators should focus on platforms demonstrating innovation, user engagement growth, and sustainable monetization strategies.

The next 6-12 months will be critical for many platforms as market maturity increases and competition intensifies. Success will depend on the ability to innovate, differentiate, and capture user attention in an increasingly crowded digital landscape.
