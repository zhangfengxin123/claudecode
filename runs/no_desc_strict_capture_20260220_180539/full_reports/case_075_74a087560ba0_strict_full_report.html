Executive Summary

Procter & Gamble demonstrates robust financial performance in FY2025 with 84.0 billionin total net sales across five business segments. The company maintains strong operating margins averaging 19.4%and generates substantial free cash flow of 14.6 billion, representing a healthy 17.4%conversion rate from sales.
