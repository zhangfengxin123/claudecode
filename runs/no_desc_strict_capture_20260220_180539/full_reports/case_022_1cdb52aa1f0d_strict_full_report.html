Apple Q1 2026 Financial Performance Analysis

Executive Summary

Apple delivered exceptional financial results in Q1 2026, with total revenue reaching 143.76 billion, representing a 16% year-over-year increasefrom 124.30 billion in Q1 2025. The quarter was marked by strong performance across iPhone and Services segments, margin expansion, and robust capital returns to shareholders. Net income increased to 42.10 billion, with earnings per share growing 18% to 2.84.

Revenue Analysis

Product Performance

iPhone continued to be the dominant revenue driver, contributing 59.3%of total revenue with 23% growthyear-over-year. This exceptional performance was driven by strong demand for Pro models, reflecting Apple's success in premium positioning. Services maintained its momentum, growing 14% to exceed the 30 billion milestone for the first time, demonstrating the strength of Apple's ecosystem and recurring revenue streams.

Mac revenue declined 7% to 8.39 billion, primarily due to lower sales of laptops and desktops. iPad reversed recent trends with 6% growth to 8.60 billion, driven by iPad and iPad Pro demand. Wearables, Home and Accessories decreased 2% to 11.49 billion amid competitive pressures in the wearable devices market.
