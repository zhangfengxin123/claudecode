3. Processing Efficiency: The Kimi K2 AI grading system performs efficiently with consistent processing times

5. Correlation Analysis: Processing time does not correlate with score, indicating fair and consistent grading.

6. Temporal Patterns: All submissions occurred within a short timeframe 1.5 hours, suggesting a deadline-driven submission pattern.
