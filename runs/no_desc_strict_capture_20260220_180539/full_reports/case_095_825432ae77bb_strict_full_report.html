Executive Summary

The cross-border e-commerce landscape in 2026 has become increasingly competitive, making product selection and operational tools critical for sellers to break through traffic saturation and improve operational efficiency. This comprehensive analysis compares three mainstream Amazon seller tools - SellerSprite 卖家精灵, Jungle Scout, and Oalur 鸥鹭- across multiple dimensions including data accuracy, feature coverage, ease of use, and pricing structure.

Key Findings:

  • Jungle Scoutleads in data accuracy with its AccuSales algorithm achieving 90% sales estimation accuracy
  • SellerSpritedominates the Chinese market with 1.5M+ global registered users and comprehensive Amazon-focused features
  • Oalurstands out with multi-platform support Amazon, TikTok, Temu, Shein, Shopify and most affordable entry pricing

🚀 Tool Selection Recommendations

For New Sellers

Individual/1-Person Team

Recommended: Oalur Free Version or SellerSprite Free Basic Features

Rationale:

  • Zero financial risk with free entry options
  • Core features sufficient for initial learning phase
  • Both offer AI-assisted functions for beginners
  • Low barrier to experimentation and market testing

Action Plan:

1. Start with free versions to learn data analysis methods

2. Focus on fundamental features: ASIN query, sales estimation, keyword mining

3. Participate in training programs Oalur's "3+3" or SellerSprite's live training

4. Validate product selection concepts before upgrading to paid plans
