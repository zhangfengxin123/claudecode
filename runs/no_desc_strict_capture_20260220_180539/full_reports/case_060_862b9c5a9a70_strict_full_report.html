Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive analysis of 141,610 user queriesfrom September 2025, revealing critical insights into how users leverage AI-powered document processing platforms. Our analysis uncovers three key findings:

1. Document processing dominates user intent- 17.6% of all queries relate to document manipulation PPT, PDF, Word, Excel, far exceeding other categories

2. Business workflows drive adoption- 18.8% of queries focus on business-critical tasks reports, analysis, planning, indicating strong enterprise adoption

3. User engagement shows high retention potential- Despite 55,403 unique users, the average user makes 2.56 queries, with power users generating up to 919 queries

These findings align with industry benchmarks: the Intelligent Document Processing IDP market is projected to reach 6.78 billion by 2025, with 80% of enterprises increasing investment in document automation. Our data provides real-world validation of these market trends.
