Last.fm Asia Social Network Analysis

Executive Summary

This analysis examines the Last.fm Asia social network, comprising 7,624 usersconnected through 27,806 friend relationships, with each user labeled by their primary music genre preference across 18 distinct genres. The network exhibits a highly clustered structure with strong homophily—users predominantly connect with others sharing similar musical tastes. Key findings reveal a highly uneven genre distribution, with the top three genres accounting for 52.1% of all users, while 87.4% of connectionsoccur within the same genre community.
