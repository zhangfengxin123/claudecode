HXC Preliminary Analysis 2024-2026

Executive Summary

This comprehensive analysis examines the Hong Kong Primary School Mathematics Competition HXC preliminary round results from 2024 to 2026, encompassing 33,036 award records across six grade levels P1-P6 and 520 participating schools. The data reveals consistent growth in competition participation, stable award distribution patterns, and remarkable performance by certain educational institutions. Notably, the competition has grown by 21.4% over the three-year period, with 2026 showing the highest participation at 12,275 awards. This report provides actionable insights for educators, parents, and competition organizers.
