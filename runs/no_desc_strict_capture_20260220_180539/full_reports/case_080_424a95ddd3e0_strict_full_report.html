1. Performance Alignment: 114% overall performance achievement demonstrates effective pay-for-performance

2. Strong TSR: 41% 2024 return significantly outperforms peers

3. LTI Focus: 80% CEO compensation in long-term incentives aligns with shareholder interests

3. Change-in-Control Payments: Large severance packages merit shareholder scrutiny

1. Vote FOR Say-on-Pay: Strong performance alignment and peer-benchmarked compensation

2. Engage on Pay Ratio: Request additional disclosure on workforce compensation strategy

4. Support Governance Practices: Maintain independent board oversight and committee structures

5. Request Enhanced ESG Disclosure: Detailed ESG target setting and progress reporting

12. Conclusion

RTX Corporation's 2025 Proxy Statement demonstrates a mature approach to executive compensation and corporate governance. The company maintains strong pay-for-performance alignment with significant LTI emphasis, delivered exceptional 2024 shareholder returns, and maintains robust governance practices. While the elevated pay ratio and operational risks warrant monitoring, the overall compensation philosophy and governance structure support long-term shareholder value creation.
