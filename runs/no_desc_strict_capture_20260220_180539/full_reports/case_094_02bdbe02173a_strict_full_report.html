Cross-Border E-Commerce AI Product Selection Solution Analysis

Executive Summary

The cross-border e-commerce market presents a significant opportunity for AI-driven product selection solutions. With a global trade volume of 15.8 trillion USDand Chinese exports reaching 2.75 trillion RMBannually, the market is vast and rapidly evolving. However, 60% of companies face inventory backlogs due to poor product selection decisions, creating an urgent need for intelligent, data-driven solutions.

Our analysis reveals a clear market gap: while existing competitors focus on specific platforms or data sources, there is no comprehensive solution that integrates market analysis, competitor intelligence, patent detection, and supply chain risk assessment into a single AI-powered platform. This creates a unique opportunity for a full-chain solution targeting small to medium-sized cross-border e-commerce companies with annual revenues between 1-50 million RMB.
