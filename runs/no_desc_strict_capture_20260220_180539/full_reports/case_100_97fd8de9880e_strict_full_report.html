IMO Hong Kong Preliminary Competition Analysis 2022-2025

Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive analysis of the International Mathematical Olympiad IMO Preliminary Selection Contest results in Hong Kong from 2022 to 2025. The analysis encompasses 423 award recordsacross 102 schoolsand 317 unique students, revealing significant insights into Hong Kong's mathematical talent landscape.

Key Findings:

  • 拔萃男書院 Diocesan Boys' Schooldominates with 41 total awards 13 gold, establishing itself as the premier institution for mathematical excellence
  • 聖保羅男女中學 St. Paul's Co-educational Collegeconsistently ranks second with 32 total awards 7 gold
  • Competition participation grew from 100 awards in 2022 to 111 awards in 2025 11% increase
  • Gold awards show a declining trend: 12 → 10 → 10 → 9, suggesting increasing competition standards
  • The competition continues to attract diverse talent from both traditional local schools and international institutions

Conclusion

The IMO Hong Kong Preliminary Competition continues to serve as a vital platform for identifying and nurturing mathematical talent. The four-year analysis reveals:

1. Healthy Competition Growth: Overall participation increased by 11% from 2022 to 2025

2. Institutional Excellence: A small group of elite schools consistently dominates, indicating high-quality mathematical education

3. Talent Diversity: Students from 102 different schools received awards, showing broad geographic and institutional representation

The competition remains a cornerstone of Hong Kong's mathematical education ecosystem, providing valuable insights for educators, policymakers, and students interested in developing mathematical excellence.
