Executive Summary

Western Digital delivered exceptional Q2 FY26 performance, achieving record-breaking results across all key financial metrics. Revenue reached 3.02B, up 25% year-over-year, driven by unprecedented demand for AI infrastructure and data center storage solutions. The company demonstrated remarkable operational excellence with non-GAAP gross margin expanding to 46.1% +770 bps YoY and non-GAAP operating margin reaching 33.8% +930 bps YoY.

The cloud segment emerged as the dominant growth driver, generating 2.67B in revenue 88.6% of total. Strong cash generation capabilities were evident with free cash flow of 653M +95% YoY. Management raised Q3 guidance to reflect continued momentum, projecting revenue of 3.2B +40% YoY with further margin expansion expected.

Gross Margin Expansion

Non-GAAP gross margin reached 46.1% in Q2FY26, a significant improvement from 38.4% in Q2FY25. This 770 basis point expansion demonstrates:

  • Pricing Power: Ability to command premium prices in high-demand markets
  • Cost Efficiency: Operational excellence in manufacturing and supply chain
  • Product Mix: Shift toward higher-value enterprise and cloud products
  • Economies of Scale: Leverage of fixed costs across growing revenue base

The margin expansion has been consistent across all quarters, averaging 193 bps of improvement per quarter over the past year.

Operating Margin Excellence

Non-GAAP operating margin reached 33.8% in Q2FY26, up from 24.5% in Q2FY25 +930 bps. This improvement reflects:

  • Leveraged Operating Expenses: SG&A and R&D as percentage of revenue declining
  • Manufacturing Efficiency: Yield improvements and cost reduction initiatives
  • Supply Chain Optimization: Reduced logistics and inventory costs
  • Strategic Pricing: Value-based pricing in high-demand markets
