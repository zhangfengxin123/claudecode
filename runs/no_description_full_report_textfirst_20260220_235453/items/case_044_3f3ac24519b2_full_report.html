Social Media Traffic Analysis Report - June 2025

Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive analysis of social media traffic performance from Twitter and YouTube during June 2025. The analysis reveals significant differences in user acquisition efficiency between platforms, with YouTube demonstrating a 3.2x higher conversion rate from traffic to registration 24.39% vs 7.59%. Twitter generated higher total traffic volume 646 UV vs 246 UV but lower conversion efficiency.

Key Findings:

  • YouTube converts visitors to registrations 3.2x more effectively than Twitter
  • Twitter drives 2.6x more total traffic but with lower quality leads
  • Product funnel shows strong engagement post-registration 85.71% enter editor
  • Top YouTube campaign ProSecrect drives 51% of total registrations
  • Monetization signals present but limited data on paid conversions
