The MAL score distribution follows a near-normal distributioncentered at 6.98 with a standard deviation of 0.62. This distribution pattern suggests:

  • Skewness: Slight negative skew mean median, indicating more high-scoring outliers
  • Range: Scores range from 6.05 to 9.29, suggesting quality filtering
  • Concentration: Most anime score between 6.5 and 7.5 70% of titles
  • Elite Tier: Only 5% of anime score above 8.0

The distribution shape reflects user rating behavior on MAL, where users tend to rate anime they enjoyed more positively and may not rate mediocre titles, creating this characteristic curve.

  • COVID-19 Impact: 2020 saw slight dip, followed by strong recovery in 2021-2023

Interpretation:

3. Popularity vs Score -0.43: Moderate correlation indicates that while popular anime tend to have higher scores, many high-scoring anime remain less popular niche appeal.

4. Episodes vs Members 0.28: Weak positive correlation suggests long-running series benefit from cumulative viewership, but many shorter series achieve massive popularity.

5. Start Year vs Episodes -0.18: Slight negative correlation confirms industry trend toward shorter seasons 12-13 episodes in recent years vs. longer runs historically.
