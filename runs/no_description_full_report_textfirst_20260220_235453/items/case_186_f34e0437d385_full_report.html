Netflix Content Analysis: Comprehensive Insights into Streaming Trends

Executive Summary

This comprehensive analysis examines Netflix's content library containing 8,807 titles, revealing key insights about streaming trends, content preferences, and platform strategy. The dataset spans movies and TV shows released between 1925 and 2021, with content added to Netflix between 2008 and 2021.

Key Findings:

  • Movies dominate the platform: 6,131 titles 69.6% vs. 2,676 TV shows 30.4%
  • United States leads content production: 3,211 titles 36.5% of library
  • Dramas are the most popular genre: 1,600 titles across movies and TV shows
  • Content explosion in 2019: Peak year for content additions
  • Average movie duration: 99.6 minutes, consistent with theatrical releases
  • Family-friendly content surge: TV-Y and PG ratings show strong growth
