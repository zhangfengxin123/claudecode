UK Energy Demand Forecast: tRESP Pathways Analysis 2025-2050

Executive Summary

This report provides a comprehensive analysis of UK electricity demand forecasts through the tRESP transient Response pathways from 2025 to 2050. The analysis covers three major decarbonization pathways: High Temperature HT, Energy Efficiency EE, and Heat Electrification HE, examining demand patterns across 14 Distribution Network Operator DNO licence areas and 231 Grid Supply Point GSP areas.

Key Findings:

  • Total projected demand across all pathways: 46.17 TWh2025-2050
  • tRESP - HTpathway dominates with 34% of total demand 18.33 TWh
  • NGED East Midlandsleads regional demand at 6.97 TWh 15% of total
  • Building block LctBB0152is the largest demand contributor at 24.58 TWh
  • Demand growth accelerates in the 2036-2050 period compared to 2025-2035
