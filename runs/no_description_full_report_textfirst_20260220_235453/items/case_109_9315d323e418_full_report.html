Model Limitations and Future Research

While the forecasting model demonstrated strong performance, several limitations warrant consideration:

1. Short forecast horizon: Predictions extend only 10 weeks beyond historical data

2. Regime transition risk: The model may not fully capture the implications of the 2025 price shift

3. External factors: Macroeconomic, geopolitical, and supply-demand dynamics are not explicitly modeled

4. Nonlinear dynamics: Linear time series approaches may miss threshold effects and regime changes

Future research could incorporate:

  • Machine learning approaches to capture nonlinear relationships
  • Explicit modeling of macroeconomic variables inflation, interest rates, currency movements
  • Bayesian methods to better quantify uncertainty during regime transitions
  • Cross-validation using rolling windows to assess out-of-sample performance
