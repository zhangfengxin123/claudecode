Fortune 500 Companies: Comprehensive Analysis Report

Executive Summary

The Fortune 500 list represents America's largest corporations by revenue. This comprehensive analysis of the 2025 Fortune 500 companies reveals key insights about industry dominance, geographic concentration, and operational efficiency patterns among America's corporate elite.

Key Findings:

  • Total Revenue: The Fortune 500 generates over 20 trillion in combined revenue
  • Dominant Industries: Technology, healthcare, and financial services lead in both company count and revenue
  • Geographic Concentration: California, Texas, and New York house 33% of all Fortune 500 companies
  • Revenue-Employee Correlation: Moderate positive correlation 0.70 between company size and revenue
  • Top Performers: Amazon and Walmart lead with over 700 billion each in annual revenue

Methodology

Data Sources

  • Fortune 500 official ranking data 2025
  • Company revenue and employee counts from public filings
  • Geographic data from company headquarters locations

Data Cleaning Process

  • Removed formatting characters commas from numeric fields
  • Converted revenue and employee counts to numeric types

Analysis Techniques

  • Descriptive statistics mean, median, standard deviation
  • Correlation analysis between key metrics
  • Industry and geographic aggregation
  • Visual analysis using Plotly interactive charts

Limitations

  • Data represents a snapshot in time annual rankings
  • Revenue recognition methods may vary across industries
  • Employee counts may include part-time workers
