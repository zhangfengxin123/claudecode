AI Trajectories Through 2030: Analysis of Four Plausible Futures

Executive Summary

This report analyzes the OECD's framework for exploring possible AI trajectories through 2030, examining four plausible scenarios ranging from progress stalling to acceleration. Based on comprehensive data extracted from the OECD analysis, we find that AI development follows exponential scaling patterns across multiple dimensions - compute, parameters, data, and cost - all growing at remarkable rates between 2010-2025.

The four scenarios - Progress Stalls, Progress Slows, Progress Continues, and Progress Accelerates- represent distinct paths for AI capability development through 2030. Each scenario varies significantly across nine capability dimensions, from language understanding to robotic intelligence. Our analysis reveals that even the most conservative scenario Progress Stalls maintains current capabilities, while the most optimistic Progress Accelerates projects human-level or beyond capabilities across most dimensions.

Key Findings:

  • AI compute has grown at 4.3x annually since 2010, with training compute increasing 100,000x from 2010-2025
  • Parameter growth rate of 2.4x annually has driven models from 0.001B parameters in 2010 to 80B by 2025
  • Algorithmic efficiency improves every 8 months on average, creating compounding gains beyond raw compute
  • Task completion capabilities vary dramatically across scenarios, from hours to months for complex tasks
  • Power and infrastructure constraints represent key bottlenecks for continued scaling

Physical Constraints

  • Cooling requirements exceeding available water resources in some regions

Semiconductor Supply Chain:

  • Advanced manufacturing concentrated in limited geographic areas
  • geopolitical tensions creating supply chain vulnerabilities
  • Capital intensity of new fabs limiting rapid capacity expansion
  • Specialized AI accelerator production capacity constraints

Data Center Infrastructure:

  • Suitable real estate with power/cooling availability scarce

Economic Constraints
