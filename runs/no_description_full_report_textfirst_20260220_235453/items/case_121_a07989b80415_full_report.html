Healthcare Patient Journey Analysis: Comprehensive Insights on Admissions, Outcomes, and Costs

Executive Summary

This comprehensive analysis examines 3,000 patient journeys across five hospital departments Emergency Room, Neurology, Oncology, Cardiology, and Polyclinic, revealing critical insights into healthcare delivery efficiency, patient outcomes, and cost management. Key findings indicate that 58.4% of admissions are emergency-based, with an overall 30-day readmission rate of 23.5%and average patient satisfaction of 4.0/5.0. The analysis reveals significant variation in cost efficiency across departments, with Polyclinic showing the lowest average cost €2,049and Oncology the highest €3,990. Chronic conditions substantially impact outcomes, increasing costs by 35%, length of stay by 2.2 days, and readmission rates by 8.5 percentage points.

Conclusion

This comprehensive analysis of 3,000 patient journeys reveals a hospital system performing well on many dimensions but with significant opportunities for improvement. The high volume of emergency admissions 58.4% presents both challenges and opportunities for system optimization. Chronic conditions emerge as a major cost driver and quality challenge, requiring targeted intervention strategies.

The departmental variation in cost, outcomes, and satisfaction suggests that best practices exist within the system particularly in Polyclinic that can be scaled across the organization. By focusing on the identified priorities—chronic condition management, readmission reduction, and operational efficiency—the hospital can simultaneously improve clinical outcomes, patient experience, and financial sustainability.

The balanced gender distribution, strong satisfaction metrics 4.0/5.0, and diverse service portfolio provide a solid foundation for improvement. With focused implementation of the recommended strategies, the hospital is well-positioned to enhance its performance across all quality dimensions while maintaining financial viability in an increasingly value-based healthcare environment.
