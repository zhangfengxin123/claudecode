Meta Platforms Q4 2025 Financial Performance Analysis

Executive Summary

Meta Platforms delivered exceptional results in Q4 2025, achieving revenue growth that significantly exceeded expectations and demonstrates the company's strong position in the digital advertising market. The quarter marked a continuation of Meta's momentum across its Family of Apps portfolio, with robust user engagement and monetization improvements driving financial performance.

Key Highlights:

  • Q4 2025 revenue of 59.893 billion, representing 24% year-over-year growth
  • Adjusted Net Income of 22.8 billion, with strong operating margins
  • Daily Active People reached 3.58 billion, up 7% year-over-year
  • Average Revenue Per Person ARPP of 16.56, up 16% year-over-year
  • Free Cash Flow of 14.1 billion, demonstrating strong cash generation capabilities

This comprehensive analysis examines Meta's financial performance across multiple dimensions, including revenue trends, segment performance, geographic breakdown, user metrics, advertising dynamics, and forward-looking guidance for 2026.
