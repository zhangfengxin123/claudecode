Henagliflozin Clinical Trials Comprehensive Analysis

Executive Summary

This report provides a comprehensive analysis of three Phase 3 clinical trials evaluating Henagliflozin 恒格列净, a novel Sodium-Glucose Co-Transporter-2 SGLT2 inhibitor, for the treatment of type 2 diabetes mellitus T2DM. The trials cover:

1. MonotherapyNCT03159052: 468 patients, 24-week double-blind + 28-week extension

2. Add-on to MetforminNCT04390295: 483 patients, 24-week double-blind + 28-week extension

3. Triple Combinationwith Retagliptin and Metformin NCT04667143: 778 patients completed, 24-week treatment

Key Findings:

  • Henagliflozin achieved significant HbA1c reductions 0.76-0.94% placebo-adjusted
  • Consistent body weight reductions 1.3-1.9 kg and blood pressure improvements
  • Triple combination therapy showed superior efficacy 1.51-1.54% HbA1c reduction
  • Excellent safety profile: no diabetic ketoacidosis or severe hypoglycemia across all studies
  • Both 5 mg and 10 mg doses demonstrated comparable efficacy and tolerability
