Interpretation

The moderate positive correlation 0.213 between Bitcoin price and Federal Funds Rate is counterintuitive but significant:

1. Inflation Hedge Narrative: The positive correlation suggests Bitcoin may have acted as an inflation hedge during the rate-hike cycle. As the Fed raised rates to combat inflation, Bitcoin prices also rose, consistent with its perceived store-of-value properties.

2. Risk-On Asset: Despite the correlation, Bitcoin remains a risk-on asset with high volatility, as evidenced by its performance during 2022 when rising rates coincided with significant price decline.

3. Market Sentiment Dominance: The negligible correlation -0.026 between Bitcoin returns and Fed rate changes indicates that daily price movements are more influenced by market sentiment, regulatory news, and crypto-specific factors rather than immediate Fed policy changes.
