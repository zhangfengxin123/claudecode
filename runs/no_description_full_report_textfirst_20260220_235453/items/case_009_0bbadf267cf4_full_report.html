CAP Analysis Report 2011-2025: Non-Conformity Cost Trends and Patterns

Executive Summary

This comprehensive analysis examines 591 Corrective Action Plans CAPs from 2011-2025, with a total financial impact of €3.3 million. The data reveals critical patterns in non-conformity costs, technology vulnerabilities, and quality improvement opportunities. Key findings include:

  • Total CAP Costs: €3,264,364.79 across 591 records
  • High-Cost Concentration: 17 incidents €50K+ account for 60.9% of total costs
  • Primary Cost Drivers: Claims 50.2% and Internal Anomalies 35.3% dominate
  • Technology Vulnerabilities: IEX 191 CAPs and SSMB 98 CAPs are most frequently involved
  • 2025 Trend: Early 2025 shows 2 major incidents with combined cost of €325,306.67

This report provides actionable recommendations for reducing high-cost incidents through preventive measures, technology-specific quality controls, and improved supplier management.
