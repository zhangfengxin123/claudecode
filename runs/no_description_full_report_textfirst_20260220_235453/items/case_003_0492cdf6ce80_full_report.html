Global AI Job Market Analysis 2025

Executive Summary

This report analyzes a comprehensive dataset of 15,000 AI job postings from the global job market, providing actionable insights into salary trends, skill demands, experience requirements, and geographic distribution. The analysis reveals a diverse AI landscape with opportunities across experience levels, industries, and locations, offering guidance for job seekers, recruiters, and organizations navigating the AI talent market.

Key Findings:

  • Average Salary: 115,677 USD Median: 100,000 USD across global markets
  • Experience Balance: Near-equal distribution across Entry 25%, Mid 25%, Senior 25%, and Executive 25% levels
  • Top Hiring Locations: Germany 8.6%, Denmark 7.3%, Canada 6.2% lead global hiring
  • Most In-Demand Roles: Machine Learning Researcher 17.3%, AI Software Engineer 14.7%, Data Scientist 11.5%
  • Remote Work: 50% average remote ratio across positions, indicating flexible work arrangements
  • Education Requirements: Master's degree most common 43%, followed by PhD 31% and Bachelor's 23%
