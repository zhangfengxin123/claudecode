Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive bibliometric analysis of 977 academic citations focused on image and video processing research, spanning from 2001 to 2026. The dataset reveals a rapidly evolving research landscape with strong emphasis on quality assessment, perceptual metrics, and multimedia signal processing.

Key Findings:

  • Strong Recent Growth: 673 publications 69% from 2020 onwards, with 2025 being the most productive year 230 papers
  • Quality Assessment Dominance: 36.6% of all publications focus on quality assessment and perceptual metrics

  • Collaborative Research: Strong collaborative networks evident from multi-author papers
  • Research Focus: Primary themes include image quality assessment 358 papers, image processing 331 papers, and video processing 161 papers

Conclusion

This bibliometric analysis of 977 academic citations reveals a vibrant and rapidly evolving research field focused on image/video quality assessment and multimedia signal processing. The strong recent growth 69% of publications from 2020 onwards, the dominance of quality assessment as a research theme 36.6% of papers, and IEEE's leadership in publications 44.7% all point to a field with significant academic and practical importance.

The research community shows strong collaborative patterns, with leading researchers making substantial contributions across multiple themes. The integration of perceptual models, the convergence of image and video processing techniques, and the growing emphasis on quality-aware applications suggest a field poised for continued growth and impact.

As multimedia technologies continue to advance, quality assessment and perceptual processing will remain critical research areas, with opportunities for innovation in AI-powered metrics, real-time quality monitoring, and perceptual optimization across diverse applications.
