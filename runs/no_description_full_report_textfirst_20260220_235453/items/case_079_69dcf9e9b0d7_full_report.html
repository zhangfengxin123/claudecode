Apple Q1 Fiscal Earnings Analysis

FY22-FY26

Executive Summary

Apple's Q1 fiscal earnings over the five-year period from FY2022 to FY2026 demonstrate remarkable resilience and strategic evolution. Despite a temporary revenue dip in FY2023, the company achieved its highest quarterly revenue of 143.76 billion in FY2026, representing a 16.0% growth over the five-year period. The most significant trend emerges in Apple's services business, which grew from 19.52 billion in FY2022 to 30.01 billion in FY2026 53.8% growth, increasing its contribution to total revenue from 15.7% to 20.9%.

Overall Revenue Trajectory

Apple's Q1 revenue journey from FY2022 to FY2026 reveals three distinct phases:

1. FY2022: Record-breaking revenue of 123.95 billion

2. FY2023-FY2024: Stabilization period with slight decline and recovery

3. FY2025-FY2026: Accelerated growth reaching new heights

The 5-year compound annual growth rate CAGR of 3.8% may appear modest, but masks a more dynamic underlying story. After FY2023's 5.5% revenue decline to 117.15 billion, Apple demonstrated exceptional resilience with consecutive growth years: +2.1% in FY2024, +4.0% in FY2025, and an impressive +15.7% surge in FY2026.

Conclusion

Apple's Q1 fiscal earnings from FY2022 to FY2026 demonstrate exceptional business resilience and strategic evolution.
