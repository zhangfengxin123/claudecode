Executive Summary

Microsoft delivered exceptional performance in the second quarter of fiscal year 2026, with revenue reaching 81.273 billion, representing a robust 16.8% year-over-year growth. The company demonstrated remarkable profitability improvements, with net income surging 59.5% to 38.458 billion and diluted EPS growing 59.8% to 5.16. The Intelligent Cloud segment emerged as the primary growth driver, expanding 28.8% to 32.907 billion, while Azure cloud services maintained strong momentum with 31% year-over-year growth. Microsoft maintained its position as the second-largest cloud infrastructure provider globally with a 20% market share, outpacing the overall cloud market's 25% growth rate. The company's aggressive capital expenditure strategy, reaching 37.50 billion in Q2 FY26 +7.4% QoQ, reflects its commitment to AI infrastructure and data center expansion.

Conclusion
