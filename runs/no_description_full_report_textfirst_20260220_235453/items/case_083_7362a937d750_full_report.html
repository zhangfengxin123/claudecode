US Economic Indicators Analysis 2021-2025

Executive Summary

This report analyzes three critical US economic indicators from February 2021 to December 2025: ADP National Employment data, 10-Year Treasury yields, and the Economic Policy Uncertainty Index EPU. The analysis reveals a strong positive correlation 0.93 between employment levels and treasury yields, suggesting a robust economic recovery during this period. Employment grew by 10.8% from its low point, while treasury yields more than doubled, peaking at 6.63% in October 2023. The EPU index showed significant volatility, reaching a peak of 560.9 in April 2025, indicating periods of heightened economic uncertainty.
