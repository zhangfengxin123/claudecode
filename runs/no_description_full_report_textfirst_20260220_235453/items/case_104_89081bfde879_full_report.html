Lamborghini Sales Performance Analysis 2020-2025: The Hybrid Revolution

Executive Summary

This comprehensive analysis examines Lamborghini's sales performance from 2020 through 2025, covering 141,374 vehicles sold with total revenue of 43.2 billion. The study reveals a strategic transition from gasoline-only powertrains to hybrid technology, resulting in significant shifts in market positioning, pricing strategies, and regional performance.

Key Findings:

  • Total Revenue: 43.2 billion across 141,374 vehicles sold
  • Average Price Evolution: From 351,667 2020 to 426,667 2025 - a 21% increase
  • Hybrid Era Impact: 2024-2025 hybrid models generated 17.7B in revenue despite representing only 35% of total volume
  • Regional Leadership: Americas leads with 39.4% market share 17.2B revenue
  • Model Dominance: Urus family accounts for 57.3% of total sales volume
