Y Combinator Companies Analysis: Trends, Patterns & Insights

Executive Summary

This report analyzes 2,291 Y Combinator companiesacross 13 batches from 2022 to 2026, providing deep insights into industry trends, seasonal patterns, and portfolio composition. Our analysis reveals that B2B dominates the YC landscape with 1,396 companies 60.9%, followed by Fintech 10.8% and Healthcare 9.6%. The data shows Winter batches consistently attract more companies than other seasons, and 2024 emerged as the peak year with 591 companies.

Key Findings:

  • B2B sector accounts for 60.9%of all YC companies, highlighting strong enterprise software focus
  • Winter batches host 45% of all companies1,031 out of 2,291, significantly more than Summer, Fall, or Spring
  • 2024 was the strongest yearwith 591 companies, suggesting YC's expansion efforts
  • Healthcare shows resiliencewith steady growth across all years, remaining in the top 3 industries
  • Government technology is emergingas a niche but growing sector 14 companies total
