Bank Customer Churn Analysis

Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive analysis of customer churn for a banking institution based on 10,000 customer records. With an overall churn rate of 26.70%, the analysis identifies key drivers of customer attrition and provides actionable insights for retention strategies. Our predictive logistic regression model achieved 73.8% accuracyin identifying customers at risk of churn, with contract type and customer demographics emerging as the most significant predictors.
