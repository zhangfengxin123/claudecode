Cryptocurrency Interest Rates Analysis: Inferring Yield Curves in a Bondless Market

Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive analysis of cryptocurrency interest rates derived from derivative instruments, addressing a critical challenge in modern finance: how to construct yield curves when fixed-rate lending and bonds are essentially nonexistent. Using data from the Deribit exchange between January 2022 and December 2024, we examine implied interest rates for Bitcoin BTC and Ethereum ETH across multiple maturities.

1.1 The Challenge of Bondless Markets

In traditional financial markets, yield curves are fundamental tools constructed from bond prices across various maturities. These curves serve critical functions:

  • Pricing fixed-income instruments
  • Discounting future cash flows
  • Calibrating stochastic interest rate models
  • Assessing economic expectations and monetary policy stance

However, cryptocurrency markets present a unique challenge: the absence of developed bond markets. Unlike fiat currencies with centuries of debt market evolution, cryptocurrencies lack fixed-rate lending instruments across multiple maturities. This absence raises a fundamental question: how can one discount future cash flows denominated in cryptocurrencies without traditional yield curves?

1.2 Innovative Methodology

This study proposes a groundbreaking approach: reverse-engineering yield curves from derivative prices. Instead of starting from bond prices, the methodology leverages highly developed cryptocurrency derivatives markets—specifically:

  • Inverse European optionscalls and puts
  • Inverse futures contractswith fixed maturities

Using the fundamental principle of no arbitrageand call-put parity, the authors construct synthetic zero-coupon bonds, from which implied interest rates can be extracted.

Appendix D: Glossary

  • Inverse Options: Options where the underlying is the inverse of the asset price common in crypto
  • RANSAC: Random Sample Consensus algorithm for robust statistical estimation
  • Zero-Coupon Bond: Bond paying no coupons, only principal at maturity
  • Yield Curve: Relationship between interest rates and time to maturity
  • Term Structure: Relationship between interest rates and maturity
  • Call-Put Parity: Fundamental relationship linking call, put, and forward prices
  • TVL: Total Value Locked, common metric in DeFi protocols
  • Staking: Locking tokens to support blockchain operations and earn rewards
  • Liquid Staking: Staking with tradable tokens representing staked positions
