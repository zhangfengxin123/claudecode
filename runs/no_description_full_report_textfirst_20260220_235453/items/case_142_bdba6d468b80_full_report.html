AMD Q4 2025 Earnings Analysis: Record-Breaking Performance

Executive Summary

AMD delivered outstanding financial results in Q4 2025, achieving record revenue of 10.27 billion- a remarkable 34% year-over-year increase. This exceptional performance was driven by robust demand across all business segments, with Data Center leading the way at 39% Y/Y growth. The company demonstrated significant margin expansion, strong cash generation, and continued momentum in AI-accelerated computing.

Key Highlights:

  • Record Q4 Revenue: 10.27B +34% Y/Y
  • Full-Year Revenue: 34.64B +34% Y/Y
  • Gross Margin: 54% GAAP, 57% Non-GAAP

  • Free Cash Flow: 2.08B 20% margin
