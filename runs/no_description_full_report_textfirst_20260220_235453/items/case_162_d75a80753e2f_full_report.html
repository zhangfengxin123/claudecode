BNPL Transaction Risk Analysis: Patterns and Insights

Executive Summary

This analysis examines 50,000 Buy Now, Pay Later BNPL transactions across four major providers Afterpay, Klarna, Affirm, Sezzle and six purchase categories. The dataset reveals critical insights into repayment behavior, default risk patterns, and demographic factors influencing payment outcomes.

Key Findings:

  • Overall default rate: 8.76% 4,379 defaults out of 50,000 transactions
  • Payment performance: 75.22% of transactions paid on time, 16.02% late
  • Total transaction value: 28.16 million with average purchase of 563.22
  • Risk assessment: Default rate is significantly higher than industry benchmarks 0.41-3.8% charge-off rates

The analysis identifies key risk factors including credit score tiers, purchase categories, and demographic segments that can inform risk management strategies for BNPL providers.

Conclusion

This comprehensive analysis of 50,000 BNPL transactions reveals several critical insights for risk management and strategic decision-making:

Key Takeaways:

1. Elevated Risk Profile: The 8.76% default rate significantly exceeds industry benchmarks 0.41-3.8%, suggesting this dataset represents a higher-risk customer segment or different underwriting criteria than major providers.

2. Clear Risk Differentiators: Credit score is the strongest predictor of default behavior r = -0.52, with excellent credit customers defaulting at 4.66% vs poor credit at 12.94%.

3. Purchase Category Impact: High-value categories Electronics: 1,102 avg, Travel: 1,630 avg show 2.3x higher default rates than low-value categories Groceries: 160 avg, Beauty: 159 avg.

4. Demographic Risk Gradients: Default rates decrease consistently with age 18-24: 11.38% → 55+: 6.87%, income Low: 10.86% → Very High: 6.76%, and credit score improvement.

5. Provider Performance Parity: All four providers show remarkably similar default rates 8.59-8.91%, suggesting limited differentiation in risk management practices.

Strategic Priorities:

1. Immediate: Implement risk-based underwriting with category-specific purchase limits

2. Short-term: Deploy dynamic pricing model and portfolio rebalancing strategy

3. Medium-term: Build predictive risk models using identified factors credit score, age, income, category

4. Long-term: Develop customer lifetime value models to optimize retention and acquisition

Expected Outcomes:

Implementing the recommended strategies could reduce overall default rates from 8.76% to 5-6%, bringing them closer to industry benchmarks while maintaining market share and customer satisfaction.

The analysis demonstrates that BNPL risk is highly predictable and manageable when providers leverage data-driven underwriting, customer segmentation, and portfolio balancing strategies.

Methodology

Data Processing Pipeline:

1. Data Loading: Imported 50,000 transaction records from CSV file

2. Quality Validation: Verified no null values, proper data types, and logical ranges

3. Statistical Analysis: Computed descriptive statistics for all numerical variables

4. Categorical Analysis: Grouped transactions by categories and computed aggregated metrics

5. Correlation Analysis: Calculated Pearson correlation coefficients between numerical variables

6. Risk Segmentation: Created age, income, and credit score tiers for comparative analysis

7. Visualization: Generated 17 interactive charts for exploratory data analysis

Statistical Methods:

  • Descriptive Statistics: Mean, median, standard deviation, quartiles
  • Correlation Analysis: Pearson correlation coefficient r
  • Risk Ratios: Computed as Group Default Rate / Overall Default Rate
  • Confidence Intervals: Not applicable population-level data, not sample
