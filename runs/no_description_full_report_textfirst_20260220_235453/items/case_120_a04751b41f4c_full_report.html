Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive analysis of 50,000 Buy Now Pay Later BNPL transactions across four major providers Klarna, Afterpay, Sezzle, and Affirm. The analysis reveals a healthy portfolio with 75.2% of payments made on time, 16.0% late, and 8.8% defaulted. Credit scores emerge as the strongest predictor of repayment behavior, with default rates ranging from 14.3% for poor credit customers to just 2.0% for those with excellent credit. The market shows balanced competition with providers maintaining nearly equal market share

2. Purchase Amount Moderate Impact

  • Higher amounts correlate with increased default risk
  • Defaulted loans average 53 more 9.6% higher than on-time payments
  • Travel and Electronics show highest risk due to large average amounts

3. Purchase Category Moderate Impact

  • Electronics: 9.56% default rate
  • Travel: 9.46% default rate
  • Home & Furniture: 7.82% default rate safest category

Strategic Recommendations

For Risk Management:

1. Implement Credit Score-Based Limits

Tighten approval thresholds for scores below 500

Reduce maximum purchase amounts for poor credit customers

Offer higher limits only for scores above 650

2. Category-Based Risk Pricing

Apply higher fees for Electronics and Travel purchases

Consider dynamic interest rates based on purchase category

Implement additional verification for high-value travel purchases

3. Purchase Amount Caps
