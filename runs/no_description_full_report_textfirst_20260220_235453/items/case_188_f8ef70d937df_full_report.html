This comprehensive analysis examines the relationship between NVIDIA Corporation NVDA stock and precious metals prices from February 2016 through December 2025. We explore correlation patterns, performance metrics, volatility characteristics, and provide insights for portfolio diversification and risk management strategies.

Executive Summary

The analysis reveals surprising strong long-term correlationsbetween NVIDIA stock and precious metals prices, particularly with Gold 0.934 and Silver 0.861. While NVIDIA dramatically outperformed precious metals with a 27,013% total returnversus 263-362% for metals, the strong correlation suggests shared exposure to macroeconomic factors.
