Training System Instructor Survey Analysis Report 2025

Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive analysis of survey data collected from 83 instructors across multiple real estate brands regarding their experiences with training systems. The survey reveals critical insights into system usage patterns, satisfaction levels, pain points, and preferences for AI-powered features.

Key Findings:

  • High Satisfaction: 98.8% of instructors rate their training systems as 4 or 5 stars average: 4.73/5
  • Primary Pain Point: 57.8% of instructors cite inability to view student progress in real-time as the biggest issue
  • Strong AI Demand: 78.3% want knowledge base integration, 75.9% want AI content generation
  • AI Attitude: 57.8% are very excited about AI digital humans, while 26.5% remain cautious

The data indicates a strong foundation for training system adoption with significant opportunities for AI-enhanced features and process improvements.

分析问题
  • What is the overall satisfaction level with current training systems?
  • Which training system is most popular among instructors?
