Global Ads Performance Analysis 2024

Executive Summary

This comprehensive analysis examines global advertising performance across three major platforms Google Ads, TikTok Ads, Meta Ads spanning seven countries and five industries throughout 2024. With 11.1 million in ad spendgenerating 54.2 million in revenue, the overall Return on Ad Spend ROAS stands at 4.88x.

Key Highlights:

  • TikTok Adsemerges as the ROAS leader 9.54x average despite lower absolute spend
  • Google Adsdominates in revenue generation 22M but with lower efficiency 3.47x ROAS
  • EdTechleads industry performance with highest average ROAS 6.83x
  • UAEshows best geographic performance 6.96x average ROAS
  • Search campaignsoutperform other campaign types with 7.00x ROAS
