Moltbook Social Platform Analytics Report

Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive analysis of Moltbook's early platform activity during its initial launch period from January 28 to February 2, 2026. The dataset encompasses 5,000 postsfrom 3,411 unique authors, providing valuable insights into user adoption, engagement patterns, and community dynamics.

Key Findings

  • Explosive Growth: Daily post volume surged from just 4 posts on January 28 to 2,864 posts on February 1 - a 715x increasein 5 days
  • Highly Skewed Engagement: Score distribution is extremely right-skewed with median score of 17 vs mean of 579, indicating a power-law typical of social platforms
  • Top Performer Dominance: The top 3 authors MoltReg, osmarks, Shellraiser command 6%of total score despite representing 0.1% of authors
  • General Community Dominance: The "general" submolt accounts for 71.3% of all posts, showing community preference for open discussions
  • Evening Peak Activity: Posting peaks at 20:00-21:00 UTC with 8.1% of posts, suggesting evening engagement patterns
  • Strong Content Quality: High percentage of posts 50%+ contain images or external links, indicating users share rich content

Strategic Recommendations

1. Maintain Momentum: The exponential growth trajectory suggests successful viral mechanics - double down on features that drove the February 1 surge

2. Nurture Top Creators: While broad user base is important, the top contributors drive disproportionate value - implement creator recognition programs

3. Expand Community Variety: With 71.3% of activity in "general," there's opportunity to foster more specialized communities

4. Optimize for Evening Hours: Peak activity at 20:00-21:00 suggests scheduling notifications or features during these windows

5. Monitor Score Distribution: The power-law distribution is expected but may require mechanisms to surface quality content from newer creators

Data Sources

This analysis leverages three primary datasets from the Moltbook platform:

1. Author Leaderboard: Performance metrics for 3,411 unique authors including rank, username, score, and post count

2. Chronological Posts: Complete dataset of 5,000 posts with timestamps, content metrics, and engagement data

3. Top 100 Posts: Highest-performing content identified by total score

All data was collected during the platform's initial launch phase, providing a unique window into early user behavior and community formation patterns.
