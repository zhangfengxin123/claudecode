CAP Process Flow

Understanding the CAP lifecycle is crucial for improving the overall process:

Process Stages

1. Customer Issue Detection: Identification of quality or operational issues

2. CAP Recording: Formal documentation of the issue

3. Classification: Categorization by CAP Type and 7M root cause analysis

4. Root Cause Analysis: Deep dive into underlying causes

5. Action Plan Definition: Development of corrective and preventive measures

6. Implementation: Execution of the action plan

7. Cost Tracking: Monitoring of Hours and Equipment costs

8. Closure: Formal closure with efficiency assessment

9. Performance Metrics: Evaluation of closure time and effectiveness

10. Feedback Loop: Continuous improvement based on lessons learned
