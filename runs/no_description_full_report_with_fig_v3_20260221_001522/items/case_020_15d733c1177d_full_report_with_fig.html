Heart Disease Risk Analysis: Comprehensive Clinical Data Study

This comprehensive analysis examines cardiovascular health indicators from 303 patients to identify key risk factors and patterns associated with heart disease. The study leverages 14 clinical variables including demographics, vital signs, and diagnostic test results to provide actionable insights for cardiovascular risk assessment.

Executive Summary

This analysis reveals several critical findings:

  • Heart disease prevalence: 54.5% of patients 165 out of 303 show evidence of heart disease
  • Age correlation: Risk increases significantly with age, reaching 72.5% in patients 60+ years old
  • Asymptomatic presentation: 77% of patients with asymptomatic chest pain have heart disease
  • Gender differences: Males show higher disease prevalence 63.2% vs 25.9% for females
  • Vessel count: 82.6% of patients with 3+ major vessels colored have heart disease
  • ST depression: Elevated ST depression oldpeak is strongly associated with heart disease

The analysis identifies age, chest pain type, number of major vessels, and ST depression as the strongest predictors of heart disease.
