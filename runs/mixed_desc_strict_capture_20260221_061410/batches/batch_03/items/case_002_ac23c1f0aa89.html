Background: DWTS pairs celebrities with pro dancers. Each week, judges score dances (1-10 scale), fans vote to keep favorites, and the lowest combined score gets eliminated. Two methods have been used to combine scores:

Rank method (Seasons 1-2, 28-34): Sum of judge rank + fan rank
Percentage method (Seasons 3-27): Sum of judge % + fan % of totals

Season 28+ also added judges choosing between bottom two couples.
My progress: I've completed Task 1 - developed a model to estimate fan votes and have the data with estimates ready.
Please help with:
Task 2: Compare rank vs percentage methods:

Apply both methods to all seasons using my fan vote estimates
Analyze if one method favors fan votes more
Examine controversial cases (Jerry Rice S2, Billy Ray Cyrus S4, Bristol Palin S11, Bobby Bones S27)
Recommend which method to use and whether judges' bottom-two tiebreaker helps

Task 3: Analyze impact factors:

How do pro dancer partners affect outcomes?
How do celebrity characteristics (age, industry, home state/country) impact performance?
Do these factors affect judge scores and fan votes differently?
I'll upload my data with fan vote estimates.
