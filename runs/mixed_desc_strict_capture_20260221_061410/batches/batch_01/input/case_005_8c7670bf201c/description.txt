This dataset explores how two commonly used fatigue-management strategies—power naps and coffee consumption—affect short-term alertness, mood, and self-reported productivity.

The data simulates responses from students, working professionals, and freelancers, capturing sleep duration, intervention type, alertness scores before and after the intervention, productivity ratings, mood levels, and side effects.

The dataset is designed for exploratory data analysis, regression, classification, and behavioral research, making it suitable for data science learners, researchers, and productivity analysts interested in evidence-based performance optimization.
