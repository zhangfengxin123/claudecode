Veil of Ignorance / Rawls / Frohlich & Oppenheimer (1993)

In the survey, everyone is in the same “original position”; in the game, students differ by points/percentile.

Relate shifts to the veil of ignorance argument.

Rawls claims that behind the veil people will agree on:

a redistribution regime, and

the difference principle (raise the poorest before anyone richer gains).

Frohlich & Oppenheimer test this with four regimes:

Floor constraint (floor, no ceiling)

Maximum income (everyone keeps earnings; no floor/ceiling)

Range constraint (inequality allowed but capped gap)

Difference principle

Their subjects: US, Canada, Poland (Poland late-1980s communist; continuum from individualist → collectivist).

Use their regime-choice graphs + your class data to evaluate Rawls’s two claims.
