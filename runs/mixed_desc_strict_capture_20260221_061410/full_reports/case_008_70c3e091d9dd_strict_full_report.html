Era 1: Iran-Iraq War

1988

The conflict originated during the Iran-Iraq War 1980-1988, with two major US actions:

1. Operation Praying MantisApril 18, 1988: A massive US naval operation that destroyed Iranian surveillance platforms, sank two frigates IS Sahand and IS Alvand, and severely damaged another ship. This remains the largest US Navy surface action since World War II.

2. Iran Air Flight 655July 3, 1988: The USS Vincennes fired two surface-to-air missiles at an Iran Air Airbus A300 civilian passenger plane, mistaking it for an attacking F-14 fighter. All 290 people aboard were killed. The US later paid compensation but never admitted legal liability.

Casualties in this era: 292 deaths

Era 2: The 30-Year Gap

1988-2019

Following the 1988 incidents, US-Iran relations were characterized by:

  • Persistent tensions and sanctions
  • Proxy conflicts in Lebanon, Iraq, and Syria
  • Cyber operations and intelligence activities
  • No direct military strikes for over three decades

This period demonstrates that strategic patience and diplomatic channels can contain conflicts, even with adversaries.

Era 3: Proxy War and Nuclear Escalation

2019-2025

Since 2019, the conflict has rapidly escalated through six major incidents:

2019: US cyberattack and sanctions after Iran shot down a US Global Hawk drone

2020: Drone strike killing Qasem Soleimani, Iranian Quds Force commander

2023: Airstrikes on Iran-backed facilities in Iraq and Syria

2024: Major airstrike campaign hitting 85+ targets across Iraq and Syria

2025: First direct US attack on Iranian nuclear facilities Fordow, Natanz, Isfahan

2025: Iranian retaliation with missile attack on US base in Qatar
