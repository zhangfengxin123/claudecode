Bitget Experience Officer Campaign - Social Media Feedback Analysis

Executive Summary

This report provides a comprehensive analysis of the Bitget Experience Officer Recruitment Campaign825, which generated 258,000 views and 427 user feedback posts. The campaign invited users to rate the smoothness of Bitget's Stocks & TradFi experience and share their insights on what they loved and wanted to change.

Key Findings:

  • Language Performance Gap: English posts generate 156% more engagement than Chinese posts
  • Quality Level Paradox: Premium 1-star content has the lowest engagement 4.2, while Premium 2-star leads 18.2

Strategic Recommendations

分析问题
  • What are the top 3 priorities for improving community engagement?
  • How should Bitget allocate resources to maximize campaign effectiveness?

Priority 1: Bridge the Language Engagement Gap

Problem: English posts generate 156% more engagement than Chinese posts.

Actions:

1. Community Education: Create guides on how to create engaging content for Chinese users

2. Content Localization: Adapt successful English content templates for Chinese audience

3. Cultural Adaptation: Understand what drives engagement in Chinese crypto communities

4. Incentivize Discussion: Reward posts that generate comments, not just likes

Priority 2: Realign Quality Rating System

Problem: Premium 1-star content has the lowest engagement, questioning the rating system's validity.

Actions:

1. Audit Criteria: Review why Premium 1-star content underperforms

2. Engagement Integration: Factor engagement metrics into quality ratings

3. Feedback Loop: Allow users to rate quality alongside automated systems

4. A/B Testing: Test different quality rating methodologies

Priority 3: Amplify Share Mechanism

Problem: Only 0.1% of engagement comes from shares, missing viral growth opportunities.

Actions:

1. UI Optimization: Make share buttons more prominent and accessible

2. Incentive Programs: Reward users who drive referral traffic

3. Shareable Content: Design content specifically for sharing insights, predictions, analysis

4. Social Proof: Display share counts to encourage sharing behavior

Priority 4: Cultivate Super Contributors

Problem: Engagement is highly concentrated among top contributors.

Actions:

1. Recognition Program: Highlight top contributors in newsletters and community events

2. Exclusive Access: Provide early access to features and beta programs

3. Mentorship: Encourage top contributors to mentor new community members

4. Revenue Sharing: Consider affiliate or revenue-sharing models for top performers

Priority 5: Optimize Content Strategy

Problem: Content performance varies significantly across dimensions.

Actions:

1. Content Templates: Develop templates based on top-performing posts

2. Topic Analysis: Identify which crypto topics drive highest engagement

3. Timing Optimization: Determine optimal posting times for maximum visibility

4. A/B Testing: Continuously test different content formats and styles
