Wordle Game Analysis: Player Behavior and Difficulty Patterns

Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive analysis of Wordle game data from January 7, 2022 to December 31, 2022 359 games. The dataset contains over 32.6 million reported game results, providing deep insights into player behavior, game difficulty patterns, and engagement trends. Key findings include a significant decline in player participation over the year, with February being the peak month and December having the lowest engagement. The average success rate remains high at approximately 97%, with most players solving puzzles in 3-4 attempts. Hard mode usage is relatively stable, averaging around 7-10% of games played.

Key Insights and Recommendations

Game Design Insights

1. Optimal Difficulty Balance: The 97% average success rate with most players solving in 3-4 attempts suggests good game balance. Words like "PARER" 52% success may be too difficult and potentially frustrate players.

2. Word Selection Patterns: Hardest words often contain:

Uncommon letter combinations

Repeated letters in unusual positions

Less common vowel patterns

Consonant-heavy combinations

3. Hard Mode Engagement: 7-10% hard mode usage indicates a dedicated niche of players seeking additional challenge without alienating casual players.

Player Behavior Patterns

1. Engagement Lifecycle: The sharp decline from 361K to 9K players over 11 months suggests:

Viral growth phase Jan-Feb 2022

Early adopter retention Mar-May 2022

Loyal core player base Jun-Dec 2022

2. Consistent Play Patterns: Weekday analysis shows players maintain consistent habits, with no significant drop-off during weekends.

3. Skill Distribution: The attempt distribution bell curve centered at 3-4 tries indicates a healthy mix of skill levels in the player base.

Strategic Recommendations

1. Word Difficulty Calibration: Avoid words with success rates below 70% to maintain player satisfaction while keeping the game challenging.

2. Engagement Retention: The declining player base suggests need for features to retain mid-tier players who may be leaving after initial excitement wears off.

3. Hard Mode Optimization: Consider highlighting hard mode achievements or creating hard mode-specific leaderboards to engage the 7-10% hardcore player base.

4. Success Rate Targeting: Maintain success rates in the 95-98% range for optimal player engagement and satisfaction.

Conclusion

This comprehensive analysis of 359 days of Wordle gameplay reveals a game with excellent balance and strong player engagement during its peak viral period. The data shows:

  • Engagement Trend: Classic viral lifecycle with peak engagement in early 2022 followed by natural attrition
  • Game Balance: Consistently high success rates 97% indicate well-calibrated difficulty
  • Player Behavior: Diverse skill levels with most players solving in 3-4 attempts
  • Hard Mode: Small but dedicated niche 7-10% seeking additional challenge
  • Word Difficulty: Clear patterns in what makes words difficult uncommon letters, repeated patterns

The success of Wordle demonstrates the power of simple, accessible gameplay with perfect information symmetry and daily constraints. The data suggests opportunities for retention and community engagement to stabilize player numbers while maintaining the game's core appeal.
