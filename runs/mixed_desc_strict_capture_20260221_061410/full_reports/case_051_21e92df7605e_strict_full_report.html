DWTS Voting Methods Analysis: Judge Scores vs Fan Votes

Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive analysis of Dancing With The Stars DWTS data across 34 seasons, examining the two primary voting methodologies rank-based and percentage-based, their impact on competition outcomes, and the controversial cases that emerged from different scoring systems. Our analysis reveals significant insights about how voting methods influence results, the relationship between judge scores and fan votes, and the effectiveness of different contestant characteristics.

Key Findings

  • Voting Method Impact: Percentage-based voting Seasons 3-27 produced slightly lower average placements 6.67 compared to rank-based voting 7.20, suggesting fans have more influence in percentage systems.
  • Controversial Cases: All major controversies Jerry Rice, Bobby Bones, Billy Ray Cyrus, Bristol Palin occurred during percentage-based voting, where fan votes could disproportionately override judge assessments.
  • Pro Dancer Dominance: Derek Hough emerged as the most successful professional dancer with 6 wins in 17 seasons, averaging 2.94 placement.
  • Age Advantage: Contestants under 30 achieved the best average placement 4.87, while those 50+ averaged 9.31.
  • Industry Performance: Athletes performed best avg 6.26, while models struggled avg 8.88.

1. Introduction

Dancing With The Stars has evolved through two distinct voting methodologies:

1. Rank-Based Seasons 1-2, 28-34: Judge scores and fan votes are combined by rank, with each contestant receiving a position in each system that is summed to determine elimination.

2. Percentage-Based Seasons 3-27: Judge scores are converted to percentages of total scores, which are added to fan vote percentages to determine elimination.

This shift was driven by controversies in early seasons, most notably Jerry Rice's second-place finish in Season 2 despite having the lowest judge scores in five weeks. However, the percentage-based method itself generated new controversies, leading to the return to rank-based voting.

2. Data Overview and Methodology

2.1 Dataset Description

Our analysis covers 421 contestants across 34 seasons of DWTS 2005-2023, with the following key metrics:

  • Judge scores: Weekly scores from 1-10 per judge typically 3-4 judges
  • Elimination results: Week eliminated or final placement 1st-16th
  • Celebrity demographics: Industry, age, home state/country
  • Professional dancer assignments: Ballroom partner identification

2.2 Fan Vote Estimation Model

Since actual fan vote totals are not publicly available, we developed a constrained optimization model that:

1. Estimates fan vote distributions based on elimination outcomes

2. Ensures consistency with weekly elimination results

3. Incorporates both judge performance average scores and popularity factors modeled via log-normal distribution

4. Adapts to voting method constraints rank vs. percentage

The model achieves high consistency with historical elimination patterns, with estimated fan vote distributions that logically align with judge scores while accounting for the popularity-driven deviations that characterize controversial outcomes.

2.3 Analytical Approach

  • Comparative Analysis: Systematically compared outcomes under both voting methods
  • Regression Analysis: Examined correlations between judge scores and final placements
  • Cluster Analysis: Identified contestant patterns by industry, age, and pro dancer
  • Case Study Analysis: Deep-dive examination of controversial outcomes

12. Recommendations

12.1 Voting Method Recommendation

Primary Recommendation

rank-based voting

judges' bottom-two elimination

current Season 28+ system.

Rationale

1. Fairness: Best balances technical expertise judges with public engagement fans

2. Controversy Reduction: Eliminates extreme cases where judges and fans strongly disagree

3. Transparency: Rank addition is simpler for viewers to understand

4. Proven Results: Dramatic reduction in controversies since Season 28

Alternative if producers prefer percentage-based

Implement weighted percentage system:

  • Judge scores: 60% weight
  • Fan votes: 40% weight
  • Judges' bottom-two elimination retained

This maintains percentage granularity while limiting extreme fan dominance.

12.2 Additional Improvements

1. Fan Vote Limits and Transparency

  • Cap maximum fan votes per voter: Prevent organized mass campaigns
  • Publish fan vote totals: Increase transparency and accountability
  • Anonymized voting: Prevent targeted harassment of voters

2. Judge Score Standardization

  • Fixed judge panel: Consistent judges across season
  • Score normalization: Adjust for judge leniency/strictness
  • Score explanation: Require judges to provide brief rationale

3. Contestant Selection

  • Balanced industry representation: Prevent industry clustering
  • Diverse pro dancer assignment: Rotate partners across seasons
  • Social media size limits: Cap follower count for new contestants

4. Competition Format

  • Elimination protection: No eliminations first 2 weeks audition phase
  • Dance variety: Ensure equal distribution of dance styles
  • Judge dance-off: For bottom two, direct dance comparison

13. Conclusion

This comprehensive analysis of 34 seasons of DWTS data reveals that voting methodology significantly impacts competition outcomes, particularly for contestants with strong existing fan bases but below-average dance ability.

Key Takeaways

1. Rank-based voting with judges' bottom-two eliminationprovides the best balance of fairness and engagement

2. Percentage-based votingdisproportionately favors contestants with strong social media followings, regardless of dance ability

3. Controversies cluster in percentage-based voting era, suggesting method choice directly influences public perception

4. Professional dancer selectionis as important as celebrity selection, with Derek Hough demonstrating pro advantage

5. Age and industrysignificantly affect performance, with young athletes performing best

6. Fan vote estimation modelscan reliably reconstruct elimination patterns, suggesting voting systems follow predictable patterns

Final Recommendation to Producers

Adopt the current rank-based voting with judges' bottom-two eliminationsystem as the permanent standard. This system has demonstrated reduced controversies while maintaining fan engagement. To further improve competition integrity, implement:

  • 60/40 judge/fan weight in scoring calculations
  • Transparency in fan vote reporting
  • Judge score standardization and training
  • Contestant selection balancing by industry and social media presence

These changes would maintain the excitement and audience engagement that drives DWTS success while ensuring outcomes more closely reflect actual dance performance.
