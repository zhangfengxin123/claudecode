| Testing Indicators | Static Precision of On-line Testing National Standard (GB/T 29161) | Laboratory Reference National Standard | Repeatability Limit of Laboratory Reference National Standard |
|--------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| Total Moisture      | <10%: ≤0.5%; ≥10%: ≤1.0%                                        | GB/T 211                               | <10%: ≤0.4%; ≥10%: ≤0.5%                                    |
| Calorific Value    | >12MJ/kg: ≤0.45MJ/kg                                            | GB/T 213                               | 0.12 MJ/kg                                                  |
| Ash Content        | <15%: 1.0%; 15%~30%: 1.2%; ≥30%: 1.5%                           | GB/T 212                               | <15%: 0.2%; 15%~30%: 0.3%; ≥30%: 0.5%                       |
| Total Sulfur       | 0.2%~5%: 0.3%                                                   | GB/T 214                               | <1.5%: 0.1%; 1.5%~4%: 0.2%; ≥4%: 0.3%                       |
| Carbon             | 40%~85%: 1.2%                                                   | GB/T 476                               | 0.5                                                          |
| Hydrogen           | 0.5%~12%: 0.4%                                                   | GB/T 476                               | 0.15                                                         |
